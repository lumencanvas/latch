# App-Wide Accessibility Audit — 2026-07-03 (Phase 4)

Companion to the later-50/51 **control-surface** a11y work. That pass covered the
declarative-UI control surface only (ControlRenderer, NodeView, the 4 canvas
editors, PropertiesPanel). This pass audits **everything else**: the node canvas
& wiring, the layout chrome, modals, the node explorer, and the connections/assets
surface.

## Method

A 5-agent read-only audit (one per surface) → adversarial verify of every finding
(reject not-real / already-done / out-of-scope / nit). **35 raw findings → 33
confirmed, 2 rejected.** Each confirmed finding is WCAG-grounded and cites the real
offending markup/CSS. Severity distribution: **16 high · 15 medium · 2 low.**

Out of scope (already fixed, not re-audited): `components/controls/*`,
`layout/PropertiesPanel.vue`, `_archived/`.

---

## Increment 1 — Modal/dialog focus management **[DONE]**

One shared composable, `composables/useDialogA11y.ts` (focus move-in on open ·
Tab focus-trap · focus restore to opener on close · Escape-to-close), applied to
all 8 dialogs. Each dialog also gained `role="dialog"` + `aria-modal="true"` +
`aria-labelledby` (heading `id`), an accessible-named close button, and — for the
two with live status — a `role="status"` toast. The AIModelManager toggle
checkboxes were switched from `display:none` (unfocusable) to the
visually-hidden-but-focusable pattern + a `:focus-visible` ring on the faux switch.

Dialogs migrated: `NodeExplorerModal`, `AIModelManagerModal`, `CodeEditorModal`
(keeps its Ctrl+S), `ShaderEditorModal`, `ConnectionManagerModal`, and the
`FlowTabs` rename + delete modals.

Composable is TDD + mutation-verified (7 tests; 5 mutations each confirmed red).
Browser-smoke confirmed (system Chrome): dialog role + `aria-labelledby` resolve,
focus moves into the dialog on open, Escape closes, and the toggle checkbox is
`display:block`/`opacity:0`/focusable — 12/12 checks, 0 real console errors.

**Findings closed:** #F7 (FlowTabs modals), #M11 (role/aria), #M12 (focus move-in),
#M13 (focus trap), #M14 (focus restore), #M15 (toggle display:none), #M26 (Escape
close), #M27 (Escape on non-focusable overlay — fixed by moving focus in), #M28
(save toast live region), #M29 (unnamed close buttons). **10 of 33.**

> Note: the "modals" surface findings #M11–M14 each named one example file, but they
> describe the *same* defect shared across every dialog; the composable fixes the
> class, not just the cited instance.
